I study how data and training shape what models learn.

I am a Ph.D. candidate at Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute, advised by Prof. Meng Wang. My research connects training dynamics, learned representations, and generalization, with a focus on vision-language models and diffusion models.

I combine theoretical analysis with controlled experiments to understand feature learning and develop better training methods.

Selected research

NeurIPS 2025

Contrastive learning with data misalignment

How do imperfect image–text pairs change what a model learns? We connect the training dynamics of jointly trained nonlinear encoders to feature purity and generalization, with experiments on recaptioning and neuron selection.

ICML 2026 · FoGen Workshop

Curriculum training in diffusion models

When should a diffusion model learn coarse structure, and when should it learn fine detail? We study a joint curriculum over noise levels and trainable capacity, connecting the learning schedule to feature formation and generation.
